faq

Questions

Do I need to open an app?

No. Everything runs through text messages: the daily brief arrives by text and you approve, reject, edit or trigger actions by replying. The dashboard is there when you want the numbers.

Does it post and message as me?

Yes, from your own LinkedIn, X and email accounts, and only after you approve by text. You can disconnect any account at any time.

Which systems are live today?

SMS Control System, AI Command Center / Daily Brief, AI Content Engine, Content & Audience Growth, Outreach and the Simple Performance Dashboard. Everything marked "soon" is on the roadmap.

Does it book meetings on my calendar automatically?

Prospects can only pick times that are free on your calendar, and the meeting is only added after you text yes. You get a 30-second briefing before each call.

What if I don't answer for a week?

Nothing goes out. It waits, and the daily brief keeps coming until you text pause.
